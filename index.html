Frequently Asked Questions About MRM Roofing Products

Find answers to common questions about mabati, roofing sheets, specifications, prices and ordering.

What MRM roofing products are available?

The catalogue includes MRM roofing options such as Box Profile, Tile Profile, Stone-Coated Roofing and Corrugated Roofing.

Additional concealed-fix and specialised profiles are also listed. Confirm product availability and specifications when enquiring.

Where can I buy genuine MRM mabati in Kenya?

You can enquire with Rolling Mills Web Store, an independently owned supplier specialising in genuine Mabati Rolling Mills roofing products. Contact our team to discuss product availability and ordering.

How do I choose the right roofing sheets?

Consider your building design, roof structure, preferred appearance, required sheet dimensions, finish and budget. A roofing quotation can help establish suitable product specifications.

How can I get current MRM mabati prices?

Roofing prices may vary depending on the product, profile, gauge, dimensions, finish and current availability. Send us a WhatsApp enquiry to confirm current prices and request a quotation.

Can I request a roofing quotation online?

Yes. Complete the quotation form with your product, project location and estimated requirements. Your details will be prepared in a WhatsApp message for you to review and send.

Do you supply roofing accessories?

The catalogue includes common roofing accessories such as ridge caps, valley trays, flashings, fasteners, gutters, downpipes and ventilation items. Confirm availability when enquiring.

Are different MRM roofing colours available?

Colour options vary depending on the roofing product, finish and specifications. Contact our team to confirm available colours for your selected roofing sheets.

How do the product calculators work?

Select a gauge where applicable and enter the required length or number of pieces. The calculator multiplies the displayed rate by the selected quantity.

These calculators estimate material cost only. They do not calculate roof area, sheet quantities, accessories or delivery.

How do I place an order for roofing sheets?

Send your enquiry through WhatsApp. The team at Rolling Mills Web Store can assist with product information, pricing, availability and the ordering process.
