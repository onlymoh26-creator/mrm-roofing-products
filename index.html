ABOUT MABATI ROLLING MILLS

Roofing solutions built around quality and innovation.

Mabati Rolling Mills (MRM) is a recognised Kenyan manufacturer of steel roofing and building solutions. Its roofing product range includes different profiles, finishes and designs for residential, commercial and industrial applications.

Whether you are planning a new home, upgrading an existing roof or working on a commercial development, explore the available roofing options and identify products that suit your project requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about roofing products and ordering.

What roofing products are available?

The catalogue covers five main categories: Box Profile, Tile Profile, Stone-Coated Roofing, Corrugated Roofing, and Industrial and Concealed-Fix Roofing.

Available models and specifications should be confirmed when making an enquiry.

How do I choose the right roofing sheets?

Consider your building design, roof structure, preferred appearance, required sheet dimensions, finish and budget. A roofing quotation can help establish the suitable product and specifications.

How can I get the current product prices?

Contact the team through the WhatsApp enquiry button for current prices and product availability.

Can I request a roofing quotation?

Yes. Send an enquiry through WhatsApp and provide your preferred roofing product, project location and estimated requirements.

Do you have roofing accessories?

The catalogue includes common roofing accessories such as ridge caps, valley trays, flashings, fasteners, gutters, downpipes and ventilation items. Confirm availability when enquiring.

Are different roofing colours available?

Colour options vary depending on the roofing product, finish and specifications. Contact the team to confirm available colours.

How does the roofing price calculator work?

Select a product and enter the required length in metres. Once confirmed product prices have been added, the calculator will estimate the material cost using the price per metre.

The calculator is not a complete roof-area or sheet-quantity estimator.

How do I place an order?

Send your enquiry through WhatsApp. The team can assist with product information, pricing, availability and the ordering process.
